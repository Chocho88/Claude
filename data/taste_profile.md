# Taste profile — what's ON and OFF for this operator

> A file-over-app resonance signal. Synthesis reads this and weights it heavily;
> Reflection uses it to fail off-register drafts. It learns: each round, mark
> ideas ON/OFF and append them below. This is a hypothesis refined by evidence,
> not a fixed rule.

## The failure axis (learned from rejected rounds)

Across ~7 rejected ideas, the miss was consistent and it was **not** lack of
novelty. The rejected cluster all shared:

- a **startup/product frame** forced onto content that was a *lament about meaning
  and energy*, not a market gap;
- optimizing **clever / disruptive / monetizable** over **true / resonant / mine**;
- a **VC voice**, not the operator's.

Rejected (OFF): Red Dot (trust-patronage app) · Patron Night · Anti-Vision tool ·
Steer (coach) · Patron Futures (securitize artists) · Anti-Collection ·
Live Drop. → *All "an app/market mechanism that…". Cut on sight.*

## ON direction (the correction)

- **Register is a choice, not a default.** The output may be an **art
  intervention**, a **strategic reframe / thesis**, a **personal practice**, or a
  **soulful tool** — and Synthesis must pick the register the content actually
  wants before generating. Default-to-product is a bug.
- **True over clever.** An idea that is ingenious but soulless, or off-register,
  is a miss even if novel. Prefer plain, deep, and honest to slick.
- **It must sound like him.** Voice: terse, deep, strategic (Sun Tzu — change the
  frame, don't fight on price), music-soul, melancholy-aware, anti-vanity. Art is
  cultural DNA, not content.
- **The test:** would *he* be proud to make it? Does it *move him*? Not "would it
  raise a seed round."
